Bitcointalk BBCode Extension

Quick reference for keyboard shortcuts and behavior.

  • Ctrl+B — Bold selected text
  • Ctrl+I — Italic
  • Ctrl+Up / Ctrl+Down — Increase / decrease size
  • Ctrl+Z — Undo (step-by-step)
  • Ctrl+Y or Ctrl+Shift+Z — Redo

Undo now tracks typing (saved after a short pause) and BBCode actions. Only changed content is saved; repeated Ctrl+Z will step back through actual changes. If Ctrl+Y is intercepted by your browser, use Ctrl+Shift+Z as an alternative.

Post titles on the unread/watchlist pages are styled bold automatically by the extension.
